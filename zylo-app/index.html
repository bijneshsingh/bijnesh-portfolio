What I'd do next · Concept

One screen for the weekly check‑in.

Owners come back to Zylo about once a week with two questions: what do I need to do, and is this working? Today they answer them by walking the sidebar module by module. A simple dashboard brings both answers onto one screen, in the agent's voice.

Concept · designed after the shipped scope

The problem

Approvals are scattered across modules

Drafts, failures and results live in Blog, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Listing, Reviews and Social. To finish a weekly review, an owner has to visit each one, and something waiting in one module is easy to miss.

The idea

A dashboard that asks only for decisions

Gather every pending draft, this week's schedule, anything broken and a few plain results into one view. The owner approves, fixes and leaves, usually in a few minutes.

Zylo Dashboard · first screen

01

Approvals first

One queue for drafts from every module, each with its source chip, a "Why" line and the same "Looks good" button used across the app.

02

Results in plain words

Four cards owners understand without marketing knowledge: drafts waiting, items going out, reviews answered, calls from Google. No funnels or traffic charts.

03

Fixes at the point of need

"Needs attention" names what broke and offers the one action that fixes it, like connecting Google or choosing a larger image.

04

The week at a glance

A day strip with channel‑coloured dots shows what goes out and when, with today highlighted. The full calendar is one click away.

05

Show the agents' work

A short activity feed of what the AI did on its own, such as pausing a weak ad. Visible work builds trust in an AI that acts for the owner.

06

Collapsed navigation

The sidebar collapses to icons, grouped exactly as in the app, so the stage has room to breathe while every module stays one click away.
